GAME 01 · LONG GUIDE

Call of Duty: Mobile

Call of Duty: Mobile combines fast multiplayer matches with larger Battle Royale experiences. Improving is not only about reacting quickly. Map knowledge, objectives, teammate positions and calm decisions all matter.

Start by learning one mode at a time. Learn common routes, practise moving between cover and notice which decisions repeatedly put you in difficult situations.

Extra tips and tricks

  • Do not chase every encounter; information and positioning matter.
  • Break predictable habits when opponents begin reading your routes.
  • Use teammates as information about where pressure is developing.
  • After a mistake, identify the earlier decision that created the problem.
GAME 02 · LONG GUIDE

Free Fire

Free Fire is built around fast matches, adaptation and survival. Learn to read the situation instead of copying the same plan every time.

Good positioning gives you information, a route away from pressure and time to decide what to do next. Team play improves when everyone shares useful information clearly.

Extra tips and tricks

  • Patience can give you better information before a decision.
  • Do not panic when a plan changes; adapt.
  • Practise movement without expecting every session to be perfect.
  • Use the character gallery as a visual guide to collection ideas.
GAME 03 · LONG GUIDE

Dream League Soccer

Dream League Soccer combines squad building with decisions on the football pitch. Balance matters because different positions and roles must work together.

Keep possession when a risky option is unnecessary, look for space before forcing a pass and maintain enough defensive structure to react when the ball is lost.

Football tips and tricks

  • Build a balanced squad instead of focusing on only one area.
  • Use patient passing to create better attacking options.
  • Do not send every player forward at once.
  • Vary your attacks when an opponent reads your favourite pattern.
GAME 04 · LONG GUIDE

eFootball

eFootball rewards patience, tactical awareness and understanding how players fit together. A squad works best when formation, player roles and playing style connect.

Control the tempo. Not every possession needs an immediate attack. Sometimes keeping the ball gives teammates time to move into better spaces.

Advanced football tips

  • Choose a clear style and build around it.
  • Watch off-ball movement before forcing a pass.
  • Change tempo instead of attacking at one speed.
  • Protect important spaces and avoid unnecessary chasing.
