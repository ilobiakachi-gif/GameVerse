Game 01

Call of Duty: Mobile

Call of Duty: Mobile offers fast multiplayer matches and Battle Royale gameplay. Improving is about understanding the objective, learning maps and making better decisions instead of rushing every situation. Start with one mode, learn common routes and pay attention to where teammates are positioned.

In objective modes, helping your team can matter more than chasing every opponent. In larger Battle Royale spaces, planning movement and choosing good positions can give you more options when the situation changes.

Map awareness

Check your surroundings and avoid repeating the same route when it is not working.

Teamwork

Stay aware of teammates and support the match objective.

Practice

Improve one habit at a time instead of changing everything at once.

Tips & tricks

  • Learn the objective before the match starts.
  • Change position when an approach stops working.
  • Use practice matches to learn unfamiliar maps and modes.
Game 02

Free Fire

Free Fire rewards quick thinking, positioning and adapting to the match. A useful habit is to make a simple plan, then change it when the situation demands it. Learn your controls, pay attention to the area around you and avoid making every decision in a hurry.

As the match develops, think ahead about where you can move next. Team communication and awareness can make it easier to react to changing situations.

Tips & tricks

  • Position yourself where you have more options for your next move.
  • Keep an alternative route in mind.
  • Stay patient when better information is needed.
Game 03

Dream League Soccer

Dream League Soccer combines squad building with playing matches. A balanced team and a familiar formation can make your style easier to understand. On the pitch, use passing to move into space and avoid forcing every attack.

Defending also starts with positioning. Keeping your team shape can help when possession changes.

Tips & tricks

  • Build a balanced squad with different roles.
  • Practise one formation long enough to understand it.
  • Recycle possession when a risky pass is not available.
Game 04

eFootball

eFootball lets players use teams in different modes, including Dream Team. Tactics, player roles and decision-making can all influence how a match develops. Rather than constantly changing everything, learn one system and understand how your players move within it.

Good football gameplay often comes from patience: move the ball, look for space and protect your shape when possession is lost.

Tips & tricks

  • Choose a tactic that matches your preferred style.
  • Learn what each player role contributes to the team.
  • Do not force every forward pass; build attacks patiently.
