GAME 01 · LONG GUIDE

Call of Duty: Mobile

Call of Duty: Mobile combines fast multiplayer matches with Battle Royale. Improving is not just about speed: map awareness, objectives, positioning and calm decisions make a big difference.

Start with one mode, learn the routes around the map and practise one skill at a time. The real character artwork above also gives the guide a clearer visual identity than generic stock photography.

Extra tips

  • Learn a few reliable routes instead of trying to memorise everything at once.
  • Watch your team’s position and the objective before moving.
  • After a mistake, review the decision that led to it rather than blaming luck.
GAME 02 · LONG GUIDE

Free Fire

Free Fire is built around fast matches, adaptation and survival. Good decisions often come from reading the situation instead of repeating the same plan every match.

Use the real Chrono and Kelly artwork in the character section to see how much better the site feels when the visuals actually belong to the games being discussed.

Extra tips

  • Keep a route away from pressure before committing to a move.
  • Share useful information clearly when playing with teammates.
  • Change your plan when the situation changes instead of forcing it.
GAME 03 · FOOTBALL GUIDE

Dream League Soccer

Dream League Soccer focuses on building a squad and playing through a football career. Balance matters because different positions have to work together.

Use patient passing, keep your shape and learn one formation well before changing everything. The image here is an official DLS screenshot from First Touch Games.

Football tips

  • Create passing options before forcing a risky ball.
  • Do not send every player forward at once.
  • Keep your defensive shape when possession is lost.
GAME 04 · FOOTBALL GUIDE

eFootball

eFootball rewards tactical awareness, patient possession and understanding how your players fit together. A team is strongest when its formation and playing style work together.

Not every possession needs an immediate attack. Move the ball, create space and choose the safer option when it keeps control.

Advanced football tips

  • Pick a clear team style and build around it.
  • Use off-ball movement to create better passing lanes.
  • Change your tempo instead of attacking at only one speed.
