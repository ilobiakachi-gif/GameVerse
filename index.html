Game 01 · Call of Duty: Mobile

What kind of game is Call of Duty: Mobile?

Call of Duty: Mobile is a fast-paced action game built around different ways to play. One of its main experiences is multiplayer, where teams compete on smaller maps and the goal depends on the selected mode. Another major experience is Battle Royale, where players enter a larger map, explore, gather useful equipment and try to stay in the match for as long as possible.

The important thing to understand is that every mode asks you to pay attention to more than just what is directly in front of you. The objective, the layout of the map, the position of teammates and the situation of the match can all affect what the best next decision is. The game can feel very fast, but learning to pause mentally and read the situation can make the experience easier to understand.

Multiplayer: short, competitive matches

In multiplayer, teams usually play around a specific objective. Some modes reward controlling important areas, while others focus on eliminating opponents or completing a different task. This means a player can be active and still fail to help the team if they completely ignore what the match is asking them to do. Learning the objective first gives a new player a clearer idea of where to move and why a particular area matters.

Battle Royale: a bigger map and more decisions

Battle Royale changes the pace. Instead of immediately focusing on one small area, you have to think about where to go, what resources are useful and when it makes sense to move. You may play alone or with a squad depending on the mode. The match develops over time, so being aware of your position and the changing situation matters just as much as reacting quickly.

What should a new player learn first?

01 · LEARN THE OBJECTIVEBefore trying to do everything at once, understand what the mode expects from your team.
02 · LEARN THE MAPNotice important routes, open areas and places where the action often happens.
03 · WORK WITH THE TEAMStay aware of teammates and support the match objective instead of treating every game as a solo challenge.
Main ideaFast action with different objectives and modes.
Key habitStay aware of the map and changing situation.
Good starting pointLearn one mode and one map at a time.

Guide information is based on the official Call of Duty: Mobile description of Multiplayer and Battle Royale.

Game 02 · Free Fire

What is the main idea behind Free Fire?

Free Fire is best understood as a game where decisions change as the match develops. In its Battle Royale experience, players enter a match, move through the environment and try to remain competitive as the situation around them changes. The game is not only about reacting quickly; it is also about deciding where to go next, how to use the space around you and when it is better to move rather than stay in one place.

At the beginning of a match, players have to get oriented. As the game continues, the environment and the positions of other players become more important. A good plan at the start may no longer make sense later, so adapting is part of the experience. This is why positioning and awareness are useful concepts for a new player to understand.

Movement and positioning

Your location can influence the choices available to you. Open spaces, buildings, routes and the direction of the match can all create different advantages and problems. Instead of moving without a reason, it helps to ask simple questions: Where am I going? What information do I have? What could change if I stay here? Thinking in this way can make the game easier to follow.

Timing and adapting

A match does not stay the same from beginning to end. New information can change your next decision, and the safest or most useful route can change as the situation develops. New players do not need to master every system immediately. A better starting point is to understand the flow of a match and gradually become more comfortable making decisions under pressure.

What should you focus on first?

01 · START WITH A PLANGet oriented and decide what your first movement should achieve.
02 · READ YOUR SURROUNDINGSPay attention to your position and the areas around you instead of focusing on only one direction.
03 · ADAPT YOUR PLANWhen the match changes, be ready to choose a different route or approach.
Main ideaMove, make decisions and adapt as the match develops.
Key habitThink about positioning before every major move.
Good starting pointLearn the flow of a full match before worrying about advanced details.

This overview is written as a beginner-friendly explanation of Free Fire's Battle Royale-style experience.

Game 03 · Dream League Soccer

What makes Dream League Soccer different?

Dream League Soccer combines two connected ideas: building a football club and then taking that club into matches. You are not only thinking about one match at a time. The squad you create, the players you choose and the way you organize the team can influence how the club develops over time.

This gives the game a sense of progression. A new player can begin by learning the basic controls and then slowly pay more attention to squad balance, positions and the type of football they want the team to play. The strongest-looking individual player is not always the only thing that matters; having useful options across the team can make it easier to handle different match situations.

Building a squad

Football is a team game, so the positions on the pitch have different jobs. A balanced squad gives you options when you need to defend, attack or change the way the team plays. Understanding the role of each position is a useful starting point before making complicated decisions about the squad.

Playing the matches

During matches, the goal is to turn your team choices into good football decisions. Passing, movement, defending and finishing are connected. If you rush every attack, you may lose possession. If you only defend without trying to create an opening, you may struggle to score. Learning when to slow down and when to move forward is part of understanding the game.

How should a beginner improve?

01 · BUILD A BALANCED TEAMMake sure the squad has useful players for different positions and situations.
02 · UNDERSTAND YOUR FORMATIONKnow where your players are expected to be and how the shape of the team affects play.
03 · IMPROVE GRADUALLYLearn the basics of matches first, then add more advanced squad decisions over time.
Main ideaBuild a club, develop the squad and play football matches.
Key habitThink about how players work together, not only individual ratings.
Good starting pointChoose a simple formation and learn what each position does.

The guide is designed as a simple explanation of the club-building and match-playing experience.

Game 04 · eFootball

How does eFootball work?

eFootball is a football game that gives players different ways to approach the sport. One option is to use an Authentic Team, which lets you play with available club or national teams. Another major option is Dream Team, where you build your own squad by bringing together players and managers and then take that team into different kinds of matches and events.

Because of these choices, eFootball can be enjoyed in two different ways. A player who wants to jump straight into a match can choose a ready-to-use team, while someone who enjoys building and developing a squad can spend more time shaping a Dream Team. Both approaches still come back to the same basic football ideas: keeping the ball when possible, finding space, creating chances and defending as a team.

Authentic Team and Dream Team

Authentic Team is useful when you simply want to choose a club or national team and play. Dream Team adds a longer-term layer because you can build an original squad around players and managers you select. As the team develops, choices about players and tactics can affect the way it feels to play.

Tactics and decision-making

Good football decisions are often about reading the situation rather than forcing the first option you see. Passing can move the opponent and open space. Player movement can create another option. Defending requires attention to where danger may develop, not only chasing the ball. These ideas are easier to learn when you practice them one at a time instead of trying to master every control immediately.

What should a new player learn first?

01 · LEARN THE BASICSGet comfortable with movement, passing, defending and the basic flow of a match.
02 · CHOOSE YOUR PATHTry an Authentic Team for quick matches or build a Dream Team if you enjoy squad development.
03 · DEVELOP A STYLEPay attention to tactics, team shape and the decisions that fit the way you want to play.
Main ideaPlay with ready-made teams or build an original Dream Team.
Key habitUse passing and movement to create better options.
Good starting pointPractice basic controls before changing advanced tactics.
