GAME 01 · LONG GUIDE

Call of Duty: Mobile

Call of Duty: Mobile combines fast multiplayer matches with larger Battle Royale experiences. Improving is not only about reacting quickly. Map knowledge, understanding objectives, noticing teammate positions and making calm decisions can all change the result of a match.

Start by learning one mode at a time. In objective modes, understand what actually wins the round. Learn common routes, practise moving between cover and notice which decisions repeatedly put you in difficult situations. In larger modes, think ahead about where you are moving and what your next safe position could be.

The biggest improvement usually comes from consistency. Do not change every habit after one bad game. Choose one skill, practise it across several matches and review what happened.

Map Awareness

Learn the shape of a map, useful routes and places where opponents commonly appear. Awareness gives you more time to make decisions.

  • Change predictable routes.
  • Use cover instead of moving through open areas without a plan.
  • Pay attention to the objective and your team's position.

Better Decisions

Speed is useful, but rushing without information can create avoidable mistakes. Pause briefly, gather information and choose a direction.

  • Ask what the team needs.
  • Keep a backup route.
  • Do not repeat a failed decision automatically.

Practice Routine

Give each session a purpose. One day focus on map awareness; another day focus on objectives and teamwork.

  • Practise one skill at a time.
  • Learn from repeated mistakes.
  • Stay calm after difficult rounds.

Extra tips and tricks

  • Do not chase every encounter; information and positioning matter.
  • Break predictable habits when opponents begin reading your routes.
  • Use teammates as information about where pressure is developing.
  • After a mistake, identify the earlier decision that created the problem.
  • Long-term improvement is more useful than one lucky match.
GAME 02 · LONG GUIDE

Free Fire

Free Fire is built around fast matches, adaptation and survival. Each match develops differently, so the strongest habit is learning how to read the situation rather than copying exactly the same plan every time. Early on, get organised, understand the area around you and avoid making decisions only because the action feels fast.

As a match changes, positioning becomes more important. A good position gives you options: information about the area, a route away from pressure and time to decide what to do next. Team play also improves when everyone shares useful information clearly and adapts when the original plan no longer works.

Use practice sessions to improve one area at a time. Focus on movement, awareness or decision-making instead of judging every match only by the final result.

Character Collection

Explore the visual side of the game through character styles, bundles and special-event identities. Different characters can make the collection side of a game as interesting as the matches themselves.

Movement & Position

Before moving, think about what you will do if the first route becomes difficult. Avoid standing exposed without a reason and keep more than one option when possible.

Team Awareness

Watch the team's direction, share useful information and avoid insisting on one plan when the situation changes.

Extra tips and tricks

  • Patience can give you better information before a decision.
  • Do not panic when a plan changes; adapt to what you can observe.
  • Practise movement without expecting every session to be perfect.
  • Review the moment before a mistake to understand what caused it.
  • Use the character and event galleries as a visual guide to the game's collection side.
GAME 03 · LONG GUIDE

Dream League Soccer

Dream League Soccer combines squad building with decisions on the football pitch. A strong team is not only about collecting famous or highly rated players. Balance matters because different positions and roles must work together.

During a match, use simple football ideas. Keep possession when a risky option is unnecessary, look for space before forcing a pass and maintain enough defensive structure to react when the ball is lost. Learn one formation properly before changing it repeatedly.

When you lose, look for the real cause. Was the problem passing, positioning, attacking too quickly or pulling defenders out of shape? That makes the next practice session more useful.

Football tips and tricks

  • Build a balanced squad instead of focusing on only one area.
  • Use patient passing to create better attacking options.
  • Do not send every player forward at once.
  • Vary your attacks when an opponent reads your favourite pattern.
  • Keep your defensive shape and react patiently.
GAME 04 · LONG GUIDE

eFootball

eFootball rewards patience, tactical awareness and understanding how players fit together. Whether you use Authentic Team or build a Dream Team, a squad works best when the formation, player roles and playing style connect.

On the pitch, control the tempo. Not every possession needs an immediate attack. Sometimes keeping the ball gives teammates time to move into better spaces. Defending also requires discipline because pulling players away from important positions too early can create openings.

Make changes for a reason. Learn what your team does well, identify a weakness and adjust gradually instead of rebuilding everything after one difficult match.

Advanced football tips

  • Choose a clear style and build around it.
  • Watch off-ball movement before forcing a pass.
  • Change tempo instead of attacking at one speed.
  • Protect important spaces and avoid unnecessary chasing.
  • Study repeated match problems before changing your entire squad.
