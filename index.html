Call of Duty: MobileThink fast · watch the map · help your team
Game 01

What is it?

Call of Duty: Mobile has different ways to play. Multiplayer focuses on team objectives, while Battle Royale uses a larger map and adds exploration and survival elements.

01 · KNOW THE MODEUnderstand the objective before rushing into action.
02 · READ THE MAPPay attention to where teammates and opponents may be.
03 · PLAY AS A TEAMSupport the objective instead of treating every moment as a solo match.

What is it?

Free Fire is built around Battle Royale and other modes. In a Battle Royale match, smart movement, timing and positioning matter as the match develops.

01 · START SMARTGet oriented and decide where your team should move.
02 · WATCH THE AREAStay aware of the changing match and your position.
03 · SURVIVE & ADAPTChoose your next move based on what is happening around you.
