Games explained in simple words

These sections answer the questions a new player usually has: What is it? What do I do? What should I learn first?

CALL OF DUTY
Mobile · Multiplayer · Battle Royale

A fast first-person action game. The goal changes by mode, but you usually need quick decisions, awareness and teamwork.

Game 01

What is Call of Duty?

In simple words: you play matches against other players. You move around a map, complete the mode's objective and try to help your team win.

What makes it fun? Matches can be fast and different every time. Multiplayer focuses on short team battles, while Battle Royale adds a bigger map, looting and survival.

What should you learn first?

Learn the map, watch what is happening around you, communicate with teammates and stay calm when the match gets busy.

Main ideaFight + complete the objective
Important skillAwareness
Best first stepLearn one map well
Game 02

What is Free Fire?

In simple words: many players enter one match and try to be the last player or team standing.

You land, collect useful items, move with the safe area and decide when to fight and when to move away. Good positioning can make a big difference.

What should you learn first?

Learn how the safe area works, choose safe places to land, collect what you need and avoid taking unnecessary risks.

Main ideaLoot + move + survive
Important skillPositioning
Best first stepLearn safe rotations

DREAM LEAGUE SOCCER
Build · Train · Compete

Build your own football club, develop players and improve your team over time.

Game 03

What is Dream League Soccer?

In simple words: you manage a football club and also play the matches. You choose your players, improve the squad and decide how your team should play.

The fun comes from building a team that fits together. A balanced squad can be more useful than simply filling every position with the highest-rated player you have.

What should you learn first?

Understand your formation, know the job of each position and build a squad with enough players for different situations.

Main ideaBuild + improve + win
Important skillTeam balance
Best first stepChoose a formation
Game 04

What is eFootball?

In simple words: it is a competitive football game where you control the team during matches and try to outplay the opponent.

You use passing, movement, defending and finishing to create chances. The key is not to rush every attack; reading the match and making good decisions matters.

What should you learn first?

Practice short passing, learn how to defend space and look for open teammates before forcing a difficult pass.

Main ideaPass + create + defend
Important skillDecision making
Best first stepKeep possession
