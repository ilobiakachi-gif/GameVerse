Profile 01 · Tactical FPS

What is Call of Duty?

Call of Duty is a fast first-person action series built around weapon control, movement, map awareness and teamwork. Different modes change the goal, but good decisions consistently matter.

Objective modes reward coordination. Search & Destroy-style rounds reward information and patience. Battle royale modes add looting, rotations and longer-term survival decisions.

Core loopFight · adapt · objective
Key skillAim + movement
Best habitWatch the map
🎯 AimLearn recoil and practice controlled shots with your regular weapons.
🗺️ Map senseRemember common routes, power positions and timing windows.
🤝 Team playShare information and play objectives instead of chasing every elimination.
Profile 02 · Battle Royale

What is Free Fire?

Free Fire is a battle royale focused on surviving while the playable area becomes smaller. Quick looting, smart positioning, rotations and knowing when to fight are central to a strong match.

Character abilities and loadout choices can change your approach, but information and cover remain important.

Core loopLoot · rotate · survive
Key skillPositioning
Best habitPlan ahead
🔥 LandingChoose a drop that gives you resources for the plan you want.
🧭 RotationsMove early when the zone changes and avoid unnecessary open-ground fights.
🛡️ SurvivalYou do not need every fight. Cover and information are valuable resources.
Profile 03 · Football + Club Building

What is Dream League Soccer?

Dream League Soccer combines football matches with club building. You can shape a squad, develop players, make recruitment choices and build a team identity over time.

A balanced team is more useful than simply collecting high-rated names. Formation, roles, stamina, passing options and defensive cover all affect the squad.

Core loopBuild · train · compete
Key skillTeam balance
Best habitFix weak areas
⚽ FormationChoose a shape that matches your strongest players and gives passing options.
📈 TrainingImprove attributes that fit a player's role.
🧠 TransfersPrioritize weak positions and useful squad depth.
Profile 04 · Competitive Football

What is eFootball?

eFootball is a competitive football experience where possession, passing, defending, dribbling and finishing all connect. Small tactical choices create or remove space.

Strong play is not about sprinting forward every possession. Read the opponent, move the ball, protect dangerous areas and choose the right moment to attack.

Core loopPossess · create · defend
Key skillDecision making
Best habitStay patient
🎯 PassingUse combinations to move defenders before attacking open space.
🛡️ DefendingProtect dangerous space before rushing at the ball.
⚡ FinishingCreate a clear angle before shooting.
